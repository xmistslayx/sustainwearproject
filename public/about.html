About us

SustainWear is a non profit organisation focused on reducing clothes waste while also helping communities by providing second hand clothes.

Our goals

Here at sustainwear we have a variety of goals which are integral to the identity of our organisation. Some of these include:

  • Our aim to reduce clothing waste by taking in old clothes and give them a second life
  • Our aim to provide those in need with clothing
  • Our aim to become a a gold standard in the industry for recycling and reusing clothes
  • Our aim to build a distrobution network for receiving clothing donations and handing them out to the places that need them the most
  • Our aim to fight against fast fashion overproducing clothes which end up in landfills.

Our motivations

Every year in the UK hundreds of thounsands of tonnes of usable clothes go into the landfills. However millions of people withing the UK are in clothes poverty. We want this to end so we plan on reducing waste by becoming a middleman beetween people and the landfills. We aim to sort donated clothing into items which can be re used, items which can be recycled in the creation of new textile goods and items which are to be disposed of in an eco friendly manner.

How are we going to achive this?

We plan to achive this by creating the website your looking at right now. We see this site as a powerful tool in our fight to cut waste because it allows for you to easily interact with us and by choosing to donate old clothes to use regardless of what state of wear they may be in your doing your part to help. We want to make this as easy as a proccess for you as we can this is why we will come to your home or a local collection center to recive your old garments. We are already having a positive impact in lots of communities. So click the button bellow to donate now.
